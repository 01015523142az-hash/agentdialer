Why abandonment is zero

Your browser places the call, so you are on the line before the other phone rings. There is no predictive dialler originating calls server-side, which is what makes the FCC abandonment rate structurally zero rather than merely low.

Calling hours

Numbers are checked against the recipient's own local time from their area code, and a call outside 8:00am–8:59pm there is refused before it is placed.
